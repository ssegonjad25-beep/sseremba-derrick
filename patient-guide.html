Your health journey

A Helpful Guide for Every Patient

Learn how to prepare for a hospital visit, organise your documents, understand referrals, communicate with healthcare professionals and take an active role in your care.

Before arriving

Prepare for Your Visit

Good preparation can help make a healthcare visit more organised.

▤

Organise Your Documents

Prepare relevant information: referral letter, previous medical records, a list of medicines you take, and any identification requested by the hospital.

◷

Plan Your Journey

Allow time for travel and possible queues. Check transport, confirm the relevant clinic and ask whether you need to arrive at a particular time.

✚

Prepare Your Questions

Write down questions, explain concerns clearly, ask for clarification and share relevant allergies and medicines with clinical staff.

↗

Understand Referrals

Bring your referral letter if you have one, keep relevant test results, ask where to report on arrival and confirm follow-up arrangements.

♡

Support Children & Dependants

Bring relevant health records, prepare an accompanying adult, bring essential care items and tell staff if mobility or communication support is needed.

!

Know When Not to Wait

Seek urgent help for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms. Do not wait for a routine appointment in an emergency.

At the hospital

What to Expect

A General Patient Journey

  • Arrival: Ask staff where to register or report.
  • Registration: Provide requested information through the approved process.
  • Initial assessment: Staff may check your needs and direct you to the right service.
  • Consultation: Explain concerns and ask questions about your care.
  • Next steps: Follow instructions for tests, treatment, referrals or review.
Take an active role

Your Rights & Responsibilities

✓

Respect and dignity. Expect courteous treatment without discrimination.

✓

Clear explanations. Ask staff to explain your condition and care in language you understand.

✓

Privacy and confidentiality. Your health information should be handled appropriately.

✓

Informed participation. Ask questions and discuss concerns before making healthcare decisions.

✓

Shared responsibility. Provide accurate information and follow the care plan agreed with your team.

Common questions

Patient Guide FAQs

General answers to common questions.

Do I need a referral letter?

Requirements depend on the service and your circumstances. If you have been referred from another facility, bring the referral letter and relevant records. Confirm whether a referral is required for your intended service.

What documents should I bring?

Consider bringing requested identification, referral documents, previous relevant records and a list of medicines you take. Check the hospital's current requirements before your visit.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the appointment form on our website to submit a request. Our team will review and contact you to confirm the details.

What should I do if I need urgent medical attention?

Do not wait for a routine appointment or a reply to a website enquiry. Seek urgent medical assistance through local emergency services or the nearest appropriate emergency facility.

What if I do not understand my treatment instructions?

Ask your healthcare professional to explain the instructions again. Before leaving, make sure you understand any medicines, follow-up arrangements and warning signs to watch for.
