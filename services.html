Your Hospital Visit

Understanding the Patient Journey

1️⃣

Registration

Ask the appropriate reception or registration desk where to begin your visit.

2️⃣

Assessment

A healthcare worker assesses your needs and directs you to the appropriate care area.

3️⃣

Investigation & Care

Depending on your condition, you may receive treatment, tests, medicines, or specialist review.

4️⃣

Follow-up

Follow the clinician's instructions about medicines, review dates, referrals, or discharge.
