Before You Visit

Useful Department Guidance

📄

Bring Relevant Documents

Where applicable, bring referral letters, previous medical records, test results, identification and appointment information.

🧭

Ask for Directions

Confirm the location of the required department with authorised hospital staff rather than assuming every service is in the same building.

✅

Follow Clinical Advice

Follow instructions from qualified healthcare professionals regarding investigations, medicines, treatment, referrals and follow-up.
